Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who We Are

This website (the “Site”) is operated by Alice Kroytor, a multidisciplinary designer based in Israel. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, shared and protected when you visit the Site or contact us.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we may receive information that you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the Site and its service providers. This may include your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring pages, pages visited and approximate location derived from your IP address.

This information may be collected through cookies and similar technologies used by analytics, advertising and website-delivery services. For more information, please see our Cookie Policy.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information:

  • To respond to messages, inquiries and project requests
  • To provide, maintain and secure the Site
  • To understand how visitors use the Site and improve its content
  • To measure the performance of marketing and advertising campaigns
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we process personal information on one or more of the following grounds:

  • Consent — for non-essential analytics and advertising technologies, where consent is required
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure and improve the Site and respond to inquiries
  • Pre-contractual or contractual necessity — when you contact us about a potential project or service
  • Legal obligations — where processing is required by applicable law

You may withdraw your consent at any time through the cookie settings available on the Site or by contacting us. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies for essential website functions, analytics and advertising measurement.

Where required by law, non-essential analytics and advertising technologies are not activated until you provide consent. You can accept, reject or change your preferences through the cookie settings available on the Site.

Please see our Cookie Policy for more information about the technologies used and how to manage your preferences.

Third-Party Services

Depending on the features enabled on the Site, we may use third-party providers including:

  • Website and hosting providers — to host, secure and deliver the Site
  • Google Fonts — to display fonts, if fonts are loaded from Google’s servers
  • Google Analytics — to understand how visitors use the Site
  • Meta Pixel — to measure advertising performance
  • WhatsApp — to enable direct communication when you choose to contact us through the service
  • Email service providers — to receive and respond to messages

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process information outside Israel, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers, such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or other legally recognised mechanisms.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including responding to inquiries, maintaining business records, resolving disputes and complying with legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the reason for which it was collected. Information collected through analytics and advertising services is retained according to the settings and policies of the relevant provider.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, including Israel’s Privacy Protection Law, 1981, the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the UK General Data Protection Regulation, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request data portability, where applicable
  • Submit a complaint to the relevant data protection authority

To exercise your rights, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before completing your request.

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also submit a complaint to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work. Individuals in Israel may contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

Children’s Privacy

The Site is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Site, the services we use or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
